# Artwork credits

WILLIBAB_TOWN — created by **Willibab / Monsteretrope**.

The supplied Readme.txt states: "License: CC BY". The original notice is retained
unchanged in art_source/Readme.txt. No license version or source URL was supplied;
this package does not assert one.

Adapted assets in this build: grass/clover pattern samples, dirt texture,
cobblestone paving, timber walls, roofing, stone walls, windows and doors from
Tileset/TOWNS_ALL_1x.png. The original sheet is included in art_source/.
TOWN_INSIDE.png is retained as a future reference and is not incorporated yet.

Adaptations for the ATWC-1 farm demo: color reduction and recoloring for NES
palettes, conversion to 2bpp CHR, texture-density reduction, grass/path edging,
window resampling, composition into 96x64 buildings, tile deduplication and
separate day/dusk town banks. See terrain_assets.py and asset_conversion.json.
These changes are adaptations and do not imply endorsement by the original artist.
Retain this credit and the supplied license notice with the adapted artwork.

ATWC-1 project: Daniel J. Roberge (aToadWhoCodes).

## Tiny Wonder Farm
Supplied Tiny Wonder Farm artwork by Butter Milk: spring farm tilemap and spring
and summer objects. Converted to 2bpp NES foliage and forest tree tiles. Original
source pixels and hashes are retained in art_source and woodland_conversion.json.
No license text was present in the supplied Farm archive; confirm the pack's terms
before public distribution. The separate Swamp pack was not used in this build.

## 0026 adaptations
Tiny Wonder Farm: barn, house facades, shrubs and indoor stock props.
Tiny Wonder Interior: source floors, brick walls, bed, cupboard and counters.
Supplied artwork is attributed to Butter Milk; original PNGs are retained.
Tiny Wonder assets were resized with nearest-neighbor sampling, reduced to NES
2bpp colors and composed into the existing map footprints. See
tiny_wonder_conversion.json for exact source hashes and CHR tile counts.
The earlier Willibab building conversion is retained as an intermediate generator;
the final town facades in this build are Tiny Wonder. Willibab dirt and cobblestone
textures remain in use. No license text was included with the supplied Farm or
Interior archives; confirm those pack terms before public distribution.

## 0027 pack characters and animals
NPC Pack - Human Empires: CIV_10_1.png, supplied by the user. Four civilian
characters and two poses each are adapted from 16x20 source cells to 16x16 NES
sprites. No author or license document was present in the inspected archive;
this package does not invent an attribution or license. Confirm before publishing.
Tiny Wonder Farm Animals (Butter Milk): black cow and brown/white chicken sheets.
Cow frames are reduced from 26x26 to 24x24; chicken frames remain 16x16. Sprites
are reduced to three visible colors plus transparency. Exact source hashes and
tile allocations are recorded in actor_conversion.json. No image generation was
used for these adaptations. Retain the original pack terms and attribution.

## Sound effects in 0028
Original pulse/noise register sequences created for this demo. No music pack samples or melodies are embedded.

## 0029 original terrain refinements
Grass blades, dirt pebbles, paving and broad wooden floor patterns were authored as 2bpp tile data for this pass. Existing pack buildings, foliage, fences and actors retain the credits above.

## 0030 regional map
Original miniature terrain, landmarks, bridge, fields, fences and small farmer were authored as 2bpp tile data. Local-area pack artwork and previous credits are preserved. This is an original travel prototype; no Chrono Trigger game assets or code are included.

## 0033 building composition
Final town facades combine Willibab / Monsteretrope TOWNS_ALL_1x roof/wall textures with Tiny Wonder Farm (Butter Milk) openings and Tiny Wonder Interior kitchen/office furnishings. Recolored, resized, composed and converted to NES 2bpp; see building_assets.py and building_report.json for provenance. Earlier facade descriptions above describe historical passes.
